CROWNFALL: Stronghold — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

CROWNFALL: Stronghold is designed to be played offline. The app does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or device data with the developer or third parties.

Local game data

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on the user's device. This local data is not uploaded to a server. Users can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Network access

The production app does not request Android Internet permission and its gameplay content is bundled locally with the application.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults because it does not collect personal data from users.

Changes

If future versions add online services, analytics, accounts, advertising, cloud saves, or other data-processing features, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the CROWNFALL: Stronghold Google Play listing.
