CROWNFALL: Stronghold

Short description:
Build a Victorian stronghold, master city districts, and fight strategic Steampunk battles.

Full description:
CROWNFALL: Stronghold is an offline Victorian Steampunk strategy game built around city construction, combat, and long-term progression.

Place buildings carefully to create specialized districts, then upgrade them from basic structures into elite and royal landmarks. Unlock permanent building specializations, construct rare royal monuments, and increase your city prestige to earn stronger bonuses.

Train your guards and enter tactical battles using attacks, defense, heavy strikes, Steam attacks, combos, critical hits, dodges, and perfect blocks. Unlock Lady Ember, develop her hero level, and use her unique Crown Pulse ability in battle.

Progress through a story campaign with multiple chapters, stages, tactical choices, and enemies with different traits. Building decisions directly affect your economy and army strength. District links appear on the city map, and coordinated upgrades can transform districts into elite and royal districts with extra production or combat power.

Features:
• Strategic city building and placement.
• Specialized districts and building synergies.
• Permanent royal building specializations.
• Story campaign and tactical combat.
• Unlockable and upgradeable hero.
• Daily missions, achievements, and rewards.
• Crown Momentum and temporary Royal Rush bonuses.
• Local sound effects with a mute option.
• Fully playable offline.
• No ads, no user account, no analytics, and no tracking.

Build your kingdom your way and create a stronghold that feels uniquely yours.
